FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

If you have any other questions than what is listed below, please feel free to reach out to Holly and Griffin before the big day!

Is there transportation to and from the hotels to the ceremony?

Shuttle details are still being finalized, check back here soon! We'll share pickup times and return times once logistics are confirmed. We will be asking you in your official RSVP whether you plan to use the bus, so we can plan accordingly.

Is there parking for the ceremony or reception?

Yes! There will be parking available at Penobscot Bay Estate, and you're welcome to leave your car there overnight if needed.

What time should I arrive at the ceremony?

The ceremony starts at 4:00 pm, please plan to arrive by 3:45 pm to ensure you can get settled and seated beforehand.

How do I RSVP?

Please RSVP via the “RSVP” tab on our website. This tab will go live once the official invites go out!

Is there a dress code?

Yes! The average temperature in Winterport, ME in mid-September is around 51–73ºF. We'd love for everyone to feel comfortable, confident, and a little dressed up for the occasion. We're opting for a semi-formal dress code, think suit and tie for men, and for ladies, long or midi length dress.

Can I bring a date?

Any +1's will be detailed on your wedding invitation specifying where we're able to accommodate extra guests.

Are kids welcome?

We love your little ones, but are asking guests to respect our wishes to not include young children in our day. We apologize for any inconvenience, but thank you for understanding!

Do you have any hotel recommendations?

Yes! We have a hotel block at the Residence Inn in Bangor. There are also Airbnbs and several other hotels you may opt for instead, feel free to reach out to us if you have any questions!

I have a food allergy, can I make a special request?

Yes! Please contact Holly or Griffin ASAP with any dietary restrictions or allergies. You can also note this on your RSVP.
