Attire

What to Wear

We invite our guests to dress in semi-formal attire. Think elegant, light, and late-summer-inspired looks to complement our beautiful outdoor venue.

For women, this may include long or midi dresses in summer tones. For men, suits in any color with a tie.

Please note that portions of our celebration will take place on grassy areas. We recommend block heels, as pointed heels may sink into the grass. Evenings in Maine can be cool, so we suggest bringing a jacket, wrap, or shawl to stay comfortable after sunset.
